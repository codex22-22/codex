About Codex Homes

Old School Principles. New School Approach.

Built on the Front Range. Built for the Long Haul.

Codex Homes LLC was founded on a simple idea: that Colorado homeowners and builders deserve a contractor with both the field experience to do the work right and the operational discipline to deliver on every commitment.

Our leadership has worked every level of the construction industry — from site superintendent to VP of Construction at national homebuilders — across more than three decades of building on the Front Range. We've managed multi-home subdivisions, led cross-functional teams, and navigated eight Colorado jurisdictions. We know the inspectors, the permitting offices, and the trades.

When we started Codex Homes, we brought all of that experience with us — and left behind the corporate red tape. What you get is executive-level oversight and accountability at every stage of your project, without the layers of bureaucracy that slow things down.

Credentials & Experience

  • Class B General Contractor License — State of Colorado
  • 30+ years of residential construction experience on the Front Range
  • Former VP of Construction at national homebuilding companies
  • 8 Colorado jurisdictions managed
  • Expertise across custom builds, production homes, and full-scale renovations
  • Deep local relationships with subcontractors, inspectors, and AHJs
  • Based in Centennial, CO — serving the greater Denver metro area
