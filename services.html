Our Services

From custom builds to construction consulting — we bring the same level of discipline and craftsmanship to every engagement.

01

New Home Construction

We manage all phases of residential new construction — from site prep and permitting through framing, mechanical, finishes, and certificate of occupancy. Whether it's a single custom home or a multi-unit development, we bring the scheduling discipline, subcontractor oversight, and quality standards that keep projects on track and on budget.

  • Custom and production home builds
  • Permit coordination and AHJ compliance
  • Subcontractor selection and management
  • Schedule and milestone tracking
  • Budget management and cost reporting
  • Quality inspections and punch list management
02

Remodeling & Renovation

Whole-home remodels, kitchen and bath renovations, additions, and structural modifications — managed with the same rigor as new construction. We coordinate all trades, handle permitting, and keep your project moving so you're not left waiting on a contractor to show up.

  • Whole-home and partial remodels
  • Kitchen and bathroom renovations
  • Additions and structural modifications
  • Trade coordination and scheduling
  • Permit management
  • Owner communication and progress reporting
03

Owner's Rep & Project Management

Not sure if your contractor is on track? Need an experienced set of eyes on your project? We serve as your advocate — reviewing schedules, catching problems early, holding trades accountable, and making sure your project is delivered the way it was promised. You get executive-level construction oversight without having to hire a full-time employee.

  • Independent project oversight and site visits
  • Schedule and budget review
  • Subcontractor accountability
  • Issue identification and resolution
  • Owner reporting and communication
  • Change order and contract review
04

Construction Consulting

For builders, contractors, and developers who need operational help without a full-time hire. We assess your current processes, identify gaps, and build the SOPs, reporting systems, and team structures that make your operation run more consistently and profitably.

  • Operational assessments and gap analysis
  • SOP and playbook development
  • Scheduling and production planning systems
  • Subcontractor vetting and management frameworks
  • Team structure and development planning
  • Cost control and reporting systems
